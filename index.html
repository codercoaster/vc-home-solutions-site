What we take on

Four trades, one crew. If a job touches more than one, it stays under one contract and one project lead.

  1. Remodels

    Kitchens, bathrooms and open-plan rework. Layout, cabinetry, tile, plumbing and electrical coordinated end to end.

    Typical3–8 weeks

  2. Flooring

    Hardwood, luxury vinyl plank and tile. Old floor out, subfloor leveled, new floor in, baseboards back on.

    Typical2–5 days

  3. Restoration

    Water and wear damage repaired properly: tear-out, drying, drywall, texture and paint matched to the rest of the house.

    Typical1–3 weeks

  4. Upgrades

    Lighting, fixtures, trim, doors and feature walls. The small changes that make a house feel finished.

    Typical1–10 days
