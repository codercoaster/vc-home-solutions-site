# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Static HTML/CSS/JS, no build step, deployed to GitHub Pages. Must also work opened directly from disk (file://), so no ES-module entry scripts.

## Users

Homeowners in the Phoenix metro (Arizona) with a dated, worn or damaged home: a kitchen or bath to remodel, floors to replace, upgrades, or repair after water damage. Mostly browsing on a phone, comparing local contractors, deciding whom to trust with their house.

## Product Purpose

VC Home Solutions is a home restoration and remodeling contractor: remodels, flooring, upgrades and restoration. The site's job is to turn a visitor into an estimate inquiry. Success = an inquiry sent.

This build is a sales demo: it must look and behave like a working site, but the inquiry form is simulated (no backend).

## Positioning

A local Arizona crew whose before/after work is the proof. Remodel buyers fear unreliable contractors more than they want novelty; the site proves craft and process with real jobs.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Inquiry form only (no booking calendar). Simulated submit with a reference number; stored in localStorage.
- Language: English (US market).
- Service area: Phoenix valley cities (Phoenix, Scottsdale, Mesa, Tempe, Chandler, Gilbert, Glendale, Peoria).
- Top-left logo links to the site root.
- Only two cursors site-wide: default and pointer.

## Brand Commitments

- Name: VC Home Solutions.
- Off-white ground (never pure white).
- Olive / sage accent, chosen by the client.
- Direction chosen by the client: craft / artisan workshop: materials, textures, construction detail, a local crew's voice, warm and human.
- Simple and elegant; mobile responsive.

## Evidence on Hand

- Before/after images are placeholders (`images/projects/*.svg`) until the client supplies real job photos.
- Stock photography from Unsplash in `images/stock/` (see README for sources).
- Phone, email, ROC license number, years in business, review quotes and job details are placeholders. Do not present new commercial claims (pricing, guarantees beyond those already listed) without client confirmation.
